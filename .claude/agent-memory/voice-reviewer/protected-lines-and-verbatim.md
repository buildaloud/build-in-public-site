# Protected-verbatim-line gate patterns (detail file for MEMORY.md index)

## Check the protected line literally appears, not just thematically covered
2026-07-15 (draft mode, "dark-dashboard-design") — when `gateGuidance` names a specific line as a "candidate line-that-lands" and says "protect it" / "render close to verbatim," check literally whether that exact sentence appears in the draft at all — don't assume thematic coverage counts. Two failure shapes seen in the same draft:
1. **Full omission** — the protected aphorism "Lie once on a dashboard and every other number on it stops being trusted too" (empty-state beat, order 8) was dropped entirely; the paragraph jumped straight to a softer line with no flat-opinion punch above it.
2. **Mid-clause truncation** — the protected line "It marks every panel as its own instrument and never once touches a data point" (color-discipline beat, order 4) survived only as "It marks every panel as its own instrument.", losing the second clause tying the opinion back to the concrete rule.

Both are gate-worthy: fix by inserting/restoring the full protected sentence verbatim at the point the outline specifies.

## Paraphrase-drift is a third failure shape, not just omission/truncation
2026-07-15 (draft mode, "ai-automation-stack") — a protected line can survive as a full sentence, on-topic, in the right spot, and still fail this check by paraphrase: the outline's sanctioned flare line "The dashboard doesn't owe me optimism; it owes me the truth, and right now the truth is mostly NOT YET" (explicitly marked as the post's ONE sanctioned negative-parallelism line, "render it verbatim") shipped as "The dashboard owes me the truth. Right now, that truth is mostly NOT YET." — same meaning, softer rhythm, missing clause, split into two sentences. Same beat also softened a non-flare but still-verbatim-tagged ourTake: "The scorecard doesn't perform confidence it hasn't earned." (theatrical verb "perform") became "The scorecard shows only the confidence it's actually earned." (flatter accounting verb). Neither omitted nor truncated — just drifted. Check protected lines by diffing the actual clause structure/verb choice against the outline text, not just confirming the sentence's topic survived.

The flare line shipped as "The dashboard owes me the truth, and right now the truth is mostly NOT YET." — this time the entire first independent clause ("doesn't owe me optimism;") was dropped outright rather than softened, so the sanctioned negative-parallelism structure the outline explicitly called for is gone, not just weakened. The scorecard line shipped as "The scorecard shows only what it can back up." — a third distinct paraphrase of the same "doesn't perform confidence it hasn't earned" original (first drift: "shows only the confidence it's actually earned"; this drift: "shows only what it can back up").
