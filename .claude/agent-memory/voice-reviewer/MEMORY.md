### Burstiness (see `burstiness-patterns.md`)
- "That's the [X]" repeated openers spread non-consecutively → elevation, not gate (2026-07-13, confirmed 2026-07-14).
- Category-noun+colon+list triad (3 consecutive) → gate; keeps regressing on "automate-blog-writing-with-ai-agents" reviewer-roster para, recheck every draft (2026-07-14, regressed 2x through 2026-07-15).
- 3+ consecutive demonstrative-pronoun openers ("That's...", "It...") at 13-20 words → gate (2026-07-14).
- 3+ consecutive short (6-10 word) same-shape sentences, hooks or CTA imperative runs → gate; seen in hook (automate-blog post), CTA (writing-alt-text-seo-accessibility), and hook again (which-claude-model-to-use, "One of those calls runs on Opus. One runs on Haiku. Everything else runs on Sonnet.", 2026-07-15).
- Color-chip/design-token triad (repeated hex+clause shape) → gate, fix with colon-fragment (2026-07-15, "dark-dashboard-design", fix confirmed held).
- Long qualifier-stacked sentence (~60-70 words) burying a personal detail mid-sentence → gate, split so the personal clause gets its own boundary (2026-07-15, "grill-me-what-an-auditor-sees", fix confirmed held).
- Fact-dense explainer paragraph (token/color/font walkthrough) with strong specifics but zero fragment breaks across 5-7 consecutive 14-30-word sentences → gate even when content quality is high; fix via single sentence split or short fragment insert, not a rewrite (2026-07-15, "design-system-with-css-variables", seen 4x in one draft — recheck every draft). Not confined to design-token beats: recurred as a 9-sentence run in a pricing-table section (2026-07-15, "which-claude-model-to-use"). Also recurred as a 4-sentence run of external-source colon-then-explanation citations (WordPress.com/HubSpot/SEJ stats paragraph, all 24-35 words, same "[Source]: [stat/claim]" shape) → gate; fix by splitting the trailing clause of one sentence into its own short fragment rather than rewriting the paragraph (2026-07-15, "automate-blog-publishing-schedule"; fix confirmed held in a later draft of the same post — two short fragments now break up the WordPress.com/HubSpot/SEJ paragraph, don't re-flag unless it reverts to a uniform run). Pattern is general across post types — recheck every fact/source-citation-dense paragraph, not just design-token or pricing-table beats. Also recurred as a role-enumeration paragraph (6 pipeline-lane descriptions in "A [role] [verb]s [narrow object]" shape across 3-4 sentences, 18-35 words each) → gate; fix by inserting a short fragment (e.g. "Six lanes, six jobs.") right before the role list starts (2026-07-16, "hired-a-team-of-specialists", security-review-lanes beat). Same post, different beat, same day: recurred again as the review-loop/tone-gate description paragraph in "The specialist team that writes this blog" (12-reviewer/15-reviewer/5-round/tone-gate facts strung across 7 consecutive 12-34-word sentences, zero fragment) → gate; fix via short insert-after fragment ("No reviewer overrides the next check.") rather than touching the fact sentences themselves. Recheck this specific beat (fact-dense pipeline self-description) on every draft of every post — it's a repeat offender shape distinct from the role-list version.
- Per-beat gateGuidance can explicitly forbid a punch fragment in a specific beat (e.g. "What a specialist actually is": "Keep punch fragments out of this beat") and the drafter adds one anyway ("Nothing's fixed.") → gate; check per-beat gateGuidance direction before applying the default burstiness heuristic, since sometimes the outline wants the beat to stay in full sentences (2026-07-16, "hired-a-team-of-specialists").
- Model+verb+location-clause triad in a payoff/summary beat recapping a routing table → gate even when the outline's own gateGuidance explicitly warned off a "tidy parallel triad"; fix by merging two of the three sentences (2026-07-15, "which-claude-model-to-use"; follow-up same day, next draft: fix confirmed held — don't re-flag unless it reverts to 3 uniform sentences).
- Repeated predicate-frame echo across non-adjacent `ourTake`s (e.g. "[Subject] earns [noun] when/by [clause]" reused 9 beats apart) → elevation, not gate, same as the "That's the [X]" precedent (2026-07-15, outline mode, "ai-automation-stack").
- Drafter splits an outline's single flowing list-hook (asyndeton, e.g. "X does A, B does C, D does E, and F does G.") into two matched short "[subject] [verb] it, and [subject] [verb] it." sentences → gate, highest-visibility spot; fix is usually just restoring the outline's own single-sentence list (2026-07-15, "ai-automation-stack"; regressed again in a later draft, same post, same spot — recheck every draft).
- Two matched-shape sentences (below the 3+ gate threshold) → elevation only, even when the outline's own gateGuidance explicitly warned against the mold; only escalate to gate at 3+ (2026-07-15, "ai-automation-stack").
- Explicit per-beat gateGuidance sentence-count directive (e.g. "keep the scope note to two plain sentences") rendered as one sentence too many in draft → gate; check actual sentence boundaries against the literal count, not just topic coverage (2026-07-16, "rate-limiting-an-llm-so-a-stranger-cant-run-up-my-bill").

### Held opinions / hedge theater (see `held-opinions-and-hedges.md`)
- Outline says "land feeling+fact in one sentence" but draft splits into two → gate; keeps regressing on "automate-blog-writing-with-ai-agents" token-bill line, recheck every draft (2026-07-14 through 2026-07-15).
- First-person admission demoted to third-person/passive → gate, restore "I"; also watch for the restored opener duplicating a protected verbatim closer's exact clause (2026-07-14, regressed 2026-07-15 on same post, two-front failure).
- "Worth being precise about [X]:" joins the hedge-theater family with "it's worth noting" (2026-07-14).
- Outline's specific personal-stake callback swapped for a vague meta-reference ("It's the same math as the hook.") → gate (2026-07-14).
- SEO keyword stuffed as a compound-noun modifier inside an existing metaphor, breaking parseability → gate; milder version (keyword swapped into a noun phrase, still parses) → elevation only (2026-07-15, "dark-dashboard-design", gate fix confirmed held).
- Mid-sentence attribution-disambiguation clause reads as defensive fact-checker voice → gate, fix via tight parenthetical aside instead of comma-qualifier chain (2026-07-15, "grill-me-what-an-auditor-sees", fix confirmed held).
- SEO keyword phrase used as a sentence's grammatical subject ("Business automation with AI agents holds up when...", "Guidance aimed at solopreneur automation with AI finds...") → gate even though grammatically parseable; reads like landing-page copy, not spoken opinion. Distinct from the metaphor-stuffing precedent (that one breaks parseability, this one doesn't). Fix: move the keyword out of subject position (2026-07-15, "ai-automation-stack"). Recurs specifically in CTA/closing beats: "Running Claude Code subagents instead of one prompt starts there, and the only cost is..." → gate, same fix (2026-07-16, "hired-a-team-of-specialists"). Watch CTA beats on every post for this shape. Also recurs in hook/stakes beats, not just CTAs: outline `intendedBeat` text "splitting equity with a friend is a founder anxiety a lot of people quietly Google and never say out loud" puts the keyword phrase in subject position → gate, same fix, move keyword to object position ("people quietly Google how to split equity with a friend") (2026-07-16, "dividing-a-company-that-makes-no-money", outline mode). Milder version where the keyword sits as a modifier on "the reader" ("Direct address to the reader splitting equity with a friend") still parses naturally as direct address → elevation only, not gate (same post/date). Outline-stage fix does NOT reliably survive to draft: same post's draft reintroduced the exact gated hook sentence plus two more bare "[keyword] is [predicate]" copula sentences in other beats → gate all recurrences, don't assume a fixed outline sticks (2026-07-16, "dividing-a-company-that-makes-no-money", draft mode; detail in `held-opinions-and-hedges.md`). Gate boundary clarified: bare copula ("is") + generic-template feel is the tell; a keyword-as-subject sentence with an active verb and real argumentative content in the modifier (e.g. "A 50/50 cofounder split chosen to dodge a conversation just reschedules that conversation...") reads as a held opinion, not landing-page copy — leave it as a pass. Family extends past the copula "is": a defining verb like "means" trips the same tell ("Public chatbot cost control, done this way, means stacking six unclever limits...") → gate, fix by moving the keyword out of subject position into a gerund-phrase subject ("Doing public chatbot cost control this way means...") (2026-07-16, "rate-limiting-an-llm-so-a-stranger-cant-run-up-my-bill").

### Concrete specifics (see `specifics-and-facts.md`)
- Outline explicitly instructs pulling a real number from an external source (e.g. git log) and draft ships vague placeholder instead → gate, even if reviewer lacks tooling to produce the exact number itself; instruct editor to run the lookup (2026-07-15, "writing-alt-text-seo-accessibility").
- A drafter can nail neg-parallelism/hedge/protected-line guidance on every other beat while dropping an entire fact-dense beat outright (e.g. a `rendersAsProse: false` code-block beat vanishes with no heading, no fence, values only scattered thin across later prose) → gate; always cross-check the draft's `##` heading count against the outline's beat count, don't assume per-beat prose quality implies per-beat presence (2026-07-15, "design-system-with-css-variables"; follow-up 2026-07-15 same day, next draft: beat present, heading + fenced code block + all hex/percent values matched facts exactly — confirms the fix holds once flagged).

### Outline mode (see `outline-mode-learnings.md`)
- `rendersAsProse: false` on the only beat carrying fact-dense content → gate, flip to `true`; self-contradiction tell is gateGuidance describing rendered-list output on a `false` beat. Recurring 3-for-3 across distinct posts as of 2026-07-15 — always cross-check.
- Exception: `rendersAsProse: false` + gateGuidance explicitly calling for a fenced code block is correct, not the self-contradiction tell — don't flag (2026-07-15, "design-system-with-css-variables").
- Honest-limit/aside texture can live inside an `ourTake` tagged with a different `paragraphFormula` (e.g. `claim-then-evidence`) → don't gate missing formula tags if the texture is already present in prose (2026-07-15).
- All 11 `ourTake`s running the identical two-sentence claim-then-consequence shape → elevation (seeds drafted burstiness flattening), not a gate on the outline itself (2026-07-15, "which-claude-model-to-use").
- Personification/trailer-style metaphor inside `intendedBeat` text (e.g. "with money whispering behind each of you") passes hedge and abstraction checks but violates the "not a trailer" register calibration on its own — distinct failure from hedge theater or vague facts; flag as off-voice overwrought imagery and swap for a plain, literal clause → gate (2026-07-16, "dividing-a-company-that-makes-no-money").
- A `gateGuidance` ban on one beat (e.g. "don't use the near-banned 'whole X' family") doesn't cover the same pattern unguarded in a sibling beat's own `ourTake` — check every `ourTake`/`flare`/`point` against bans stated ANYWHERE in the outline, not just the beat carrying the ban → gate (2026-07-16, "teaching-a-robot-to-balance-my-game", beat-2 ourTake "the whole enabling decision").

### Protected verbatim lines (see `protected-lines-and-verbatim.md`)
- `gateGuidance` "protect/render verbatim" lines can be fully omitted or mid-clause truncated in draft → gate; check the literal string appears, not just thematic coverage (2026-07-15, "dark-dashboard-design").
- Third failure shape: paraphrase-drift — line survives as a full, on-topic, right-spot sentence but with softened verbs/clauses removed (e.g. a sanctioned negative-parallelism flare line collapsed into two plain sentences, a "doesn't perform confidence" swapped for "shows only the confidence") → gate; diff clause structure/verb choice against the outline text, not just topic survival (2026-07-15, "ai-automation-stack"). Confirmed recurring weak spot: same two lines drifted AGAIN in a later draft of the same post (flare line lost its entire negative clause this time; scorecard line drifted to a third distinct paraphrase), plus a third protected line ("A regex has no ego" → "Scoring code has no ego") literalized against explicit gateGuidance not to. Always diff these exact lines verbatim on every draft of this post.
- New drift variant: numeral-format drift on a verbatim-marked quote — "3-5 teammates is the sweet spot" (Osmani, source uses numerals) rendered in draft as "Three to five teammates is the sweet spot." Still gate even though wording/meaning survives; spelled-out numbers in a quote marked verbatim both break quote fidelity and dull the number's concreteness. Diff numeral formatting, not just words (2026-07-16, "hired-a-team-of-specialists").
