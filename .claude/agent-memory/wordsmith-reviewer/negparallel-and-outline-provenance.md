# negParallel "but" upgrade + outline-provenance rule

**negParallel "but" upgrade**: outline `intendedBeat` sometimes phrases a source paraphrase safely (colon, no "but"), and the drafter upgrades it to a "not X, but Y" negative-parallel construction that trips the tone-grader (e.g. outline "not to spam a keyphrase: work it in only when relevant" → draft added ", but to work it in only when..."). When the outline supplies a specific construction/punctuation, check the draft against it exactly — a drafter-added "but" is a regression, not a stylistic choice.

**Outline-provenance rule (general)**: any weak/clichéd word baked into an outline beat's `ourTake`/facts text (e.g. "leverage," "gets people burned," "sitting at the repo root") still deserves an elevation flag with a sharper swap — flag it regardless of outline provenance, since elevations are non-gating and low-risk. Expect these to survive synthesis more often than drafter-original instances, since the outline's own phrasing may be treated as intentional.

**One-negParallel-total cap gets violated in a *different* beat than the sanctioned one**: when a beat 1/2 `gateGuidance` designates a single flare line as "the ONLY negative-parallel construction permitted in the whole post," check every other beat too — the drafter sometimes slips a second, unrelated "X, not Y" construction into a late beat that has no negParallel guidance of its own (e.g. `hired-a-team-of-specialists` 2026-07-16 draft: beat 8 added "it stands ahead of all the generated legwork, not buried inside it," unrelated to the sanctioned beat-1 flare line). Standing check: grep the whole draft for "not " right before a comma/period once the sanctioned line is found, not just the beat it's declared in.
