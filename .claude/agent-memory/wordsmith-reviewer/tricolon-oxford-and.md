**Confirmed Scout-voice device, not an oversight**: the drafter deliberately drops the oxford "and" on 3-item lists to dodge the tricolon AI-tell (e.g. "the drafter, the fifteen critics, the deterministic gate" — no "and" before the last item). Don't flag the asyndeton itself.

Applies to imperative-verb triples, not just noun lists.
