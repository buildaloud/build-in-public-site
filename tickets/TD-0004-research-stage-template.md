# TD-0004 · research-stage template: idea -> validation brief

## Why

Ideas currently go straight to build. With ~90 min/week, killing bad ideas cheaply is the highest-leverage automation: research is where AI does 95% of the work and Chad makes one decision.

## What

Add new-project-template/research/: a CLAUDE.md that drives the existing deep-research + seo-researcher agents to produce a one-page validation brief (problem, who pays, top-3 competitors + pricing, differentiator wedge, demand signals, kill criteria). One command in, one brief out, Chad reads it in 10 minutes and decides go/no-go.

## Acceptance

- [ ] research/CLAUDE.md + brief template exist in new-project-template
- [ ] Run end-to-end once on a real candidate idea; brief judged useful
