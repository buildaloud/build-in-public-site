# TD-0007 · splashpage template (what-is-this + CTA + subscribe)

## Why

Every experiment needs a landing page on day one to measure interest before the product exists: traffic -> email signups is the first funnel metric.

## What

A template page in micro-blueprint: hero explaining what the thing is, go-to-app CTA (or waitlist state), app-bar link, Buttondown subscribe tagged with the product id. Brand-configurable per product. GA + PostHog wired.

## Acceptance

- [ ] New product splash deployable in under 30 minutes from config
- [ ] Subscribes land in Buttondown tagged with the product
