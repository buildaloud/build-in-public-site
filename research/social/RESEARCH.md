## Numbers that set expectations

- Opt-in: 2.1% average, 3-8% well-optimized, on *warm* traffic. Cold social clicks: under 1%.
- HN AI-comment ban: 4,229 points of community support. Not a gray area.
- Bluesky rate limits: a post costs 3 of 35,000 daily points — automation ceiling is cultural, not technical.
- LinkedIn: link posts lose ~60% reach → magnet links go in the first comment.
- PH featured (top ~10%): 1,000-5,000 visitors, 10-150 signups. Non-featured: 100-500 visitors, 1-15 signups.
- Referral benchmark to beat: $0.17/subscriber, 32% conversion (whop.com newsletter stats).
- csallen's first ~30 newsletter subs took 150 cold emails. There is no passive path to the first hundred.

## Hard platform rules (violations = ban)

- **HN**: no AI-written posts or comments, ever. Show HN needs a tryable product (repo/app), not a blog or newsletter.
- **Reddit**: no posting automation (Responsible Builder Policy); undisclosed AI = Zurich-scandal territory; 9:1 promo ratio, 30d/100-karma account warm-up.
- **LinkedIn**: no fake/persona profiles; no browser automation; official-API tools only.
- **Bluesky**: no automated replies/likes/follows; self-label bot; original scheduled posts are fine.
- **mastodon.social**: AI-only profiles "will not be tolerated" — instance choice is load-bearing (botsin.space is dead; a live bot-tolerant instance must be named before signup).
