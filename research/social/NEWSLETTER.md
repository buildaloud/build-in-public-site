# Newsletter blueprint — the weekly digest + flows

From the martech/ecommerce research sweep (2026-07-04). Buttondown; graded
on clicks (5%+ great, <1% red flag) and replies — not opens (unreliable
post-Apple-MPP).

## Subject + preview

- Subject: first-person narrative with visible stakes, 28-40 chars.
  "I shipped the wrong fix first." Never the word "newsletter" (~19% open
  penalty).
- Preview text: <90 chars, extends the subject as a second hook, never
  repeats it. "It cost us our first user. One redirect URL."

## Section order (5-minute read)

1. **Scout's opener** — 2-3 sentences, the only editorializing. Carries
   the week's emotional target, sets up the Big Thing.
2. **The Big Thing** — the week's War Story as narrative, 150-250 words,
   real numbers, ending "Read the full post: [plain link]". No buttons
   anywhere; plain-text links beat styled buttons with devs.
3. **Field Notes** — 4-6 curated links, 2-sentence summary + one flat
   opinion each. The opinion line is mandatory (discovery-only is TLDR's
   documented weakness).
4. **The Ledger** — one line of unspun numbers: subscribers, revenue,
   spend. Standing section; readers learn to scan for it. (Also the
   quotable stat for amplifiers.)
5. **PS** — single one-word reply-bait: "Reply with one word: shipping or
   stuck?" Replies train deliverability and are the compounding signal.

## Flows before campaigns (behavior-triggered beats calendar 3-13x)

**Welcome flow ships before digest optimization:**
- Email 1, instant: what buildaloud is, one best post, primary-inbox ask,
  reply bait "what are you building?" (welcomes average 51% opens — the
  highest-attention send there is; target 60%+).
- Email 2, day 2-3: best-of curation, real numbers as social proof.
- Email 3, day 7-10: pick-your-lane (how-tos vs war stories) — seeds the
  audience segmentation tags.
- Suppress welcome-flow subscribers from the digest until the flow ends.

**Win-back at 60-90 days inactive:** one dry note ("still shipping. want
back in or out?"), then sunset. Deliverability over vanity list size.

## Segmentation (phase 2 — needs signal first)

Buttondown tags per audience segment, populated by: magnet choice at
signup, welcome email 3's pick-your-lane, and link-click behavior. Until
tags have volume, the digest is one edition; after, Field Notes and the
Big Thing can be weighted per segment.

## Explicitly avoided (dev-audience gross-out list)

Countdown timers, fake scarcity, "we miss you" guilt, styled buttons,
emoji-stuffed subjects, open-rate worship, daily sends.

## Cadence

Same day, same hour, weekly, no exceptions — the digest is the one
channel with fixed-schedule discipline (Hub-tier rule).
